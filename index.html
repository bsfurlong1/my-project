Draft grades

Value = (pick number) − (player's rank at draft time). A positive value means the team got a player who was ranked better than where they picked (a steal); negative means they picked a player ranked worse than the slot (a reach). Grades are relative to the other teams in this draft, based on average pick value. Manual/unranked picks aren't counted toward value.
